Privacy Policy · last updated 13 November 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio

Duels against the game's own foes stay on your phone. The game sends something only when you tap Find a rival or Play a friend, and then only what the other player needs to see. There is no account, no ads and no analytics.

What is sent, and when

Nothing leaves your phone until you tap Find a rival or Play a friend. After that tap, the game sends:

  • The display name you typed. It is optional: with no name you show as "Player" (or "Rival" in Find a rival). Use a nickname if you like.
  • Your picks in each round (sealed first, then shown once both players have picked), your ready taps, and in a friend room the emotes and stance calls you tap and the room's score.
  • In Find a rival, the rating number from this phone and the time you started looking, so two players near the same level can meet.

Each open tab of the game uses a random id it makes up itself. It is not your device id or an advertising id, and it is not tied to you.

Where it goes

To the studio's Firebase Realtime Database, a Google service that stores it for the studio. It travels only over secure connections (HTTPS and secure web sockets). It is shown to the other player in your duel or room and is not shared with anyone else.

How long it is kept

  • Find a rival: your place in the queue is removed when you stop looking, find a duel or close the game. A duel's record is removed a few seconds after its result.
  • Play a friend: each duel's picks are removed a few seconds after its result. The room, with the names in it and its score, is removed when the last player taps Leave. If the last player just closes the game, the room stays until someone opens that code again, and is removed then.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, unlocks, sunbeams and settings are kept in this phone's browser storage and never sent. The name you typed is kept there too, and is sent only as described above. Clearing the app's storage deletes all of it; there is no copy anywhere else.

Other requests the game makes

The game's own files, its type faces among them, come from lucidwinds.com. These are ordinary file requests; nothing you made is in them. After the first visit the game keeps its own copies, so it plays offline.

On the website only (never in the Play app), the page also loads the Lucid Winds Sunbeam script. If you are signed in to Lucid Winds on lucidwinds.com in the same browser, a win also adds to that account's Sunbeams.

Nothing else

No analytics, no ads, no crash reports, no location, no contacts, no photos.

Removal and questions

Data sent during a duel or a room is removed on its own as described above. To ask for anything to be removed sooner, or with any question about this policy, write with your room code or the name you used:

stephen@skywolfstudio.com

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version is posted at this address.
